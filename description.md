We've all sat down to make some sort of dashboard, Power BI report, or even just a simple plot, only to be met by an ominous blank screen. So many decisions to be made! And too many options to choose from. Should I put a bar chart or a line chart here? What difference does it make? Does this data work for a pie chart? These are the questions many of us come up against routinely. In this session, we'll discuss a framework us at RAD have found useful to create thoughtful, intentional, robust visualizations that mold to the stories we want to tell. We've learned that by following tried and true principles from data storytelling and behavioral design disciplines, we can tailor visualization types to business problems at a capacity that gets users the information they need, with the context required to inform decision making where it counts.
